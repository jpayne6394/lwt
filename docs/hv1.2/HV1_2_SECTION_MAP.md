# HV1.2 Homepage Section Map

Codex must preserve this homepage order unless the user explicitly changes it.

## 1. Announcement and Header

Premium utility entry into the store. Keep this clean, calm, and readable.

## 2. Hero Slideshow / Banner

First impression and main entry point. The hero should be clean and premium. Do not place the large gold wave here. Use quiet negative space for live heading and CTA content.

## 3. Quick Links

A short navigation band for common user intent. It should feel integrated with the page instead of pasted on top.

## 4. About Living Well Today

Story and trust section. Use warm signal art in dead space. Keep copy readable. Do not make this a full dark page.

## 5. Practitioners + Booking CTA

Trust-to-action zone. Cards and buttons must be live components. The large gold wave/interlock can support the transition around this zone.

## 6. Shop by Goal

Intent-based shopping path. Keep tiles readable and use supported category handles or documented fallbacks.

## 7. Product Rail One

Real product display. Use Shopify data where available. Background art should support products, not compete with them.

## 8. Dark Featured Product Band

Premium dark commerce moment. Use a navy field with controlled gold signal art. Keep product content live.

## 9. Product Rail Two

Second shopping path. Maintain visual continuity from the dark feature band.

## 10. Wellness Insights

Education and trust content. Prefer real article data if available. Use documented fallback if not.

## 11. Small Booking CTA

Repeat action point after shopping and education. Keep warm and concise.

## 12. Footer Close

Grounded page close. Use navy, cream, and subtle gold signal accents so the footer feels connected to the page.
