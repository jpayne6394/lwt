# Safe Zone Rules

The safe-zone boxes in `index.html` and `assets/safe-zones/` are placeholders for future HTML/CSS content only.

## Rules

- Safe zones are review guides, not production layout components.
- The background images must remain free of real copy, cards, buttons, portraits, products, stats, navigation, and footer content.
- Desktop safe-zone PNGs are available in `assets/safe-zones/`.
- Mobile safe zones are shown in `index.html` with responsive CSS overlays rather than separate mobile overlay PNGs.
- All safe zones should keep content away from dense motif clusters, bright flares, and edge details.

## Marked Zones

- Hero: left headline/copy/buttons; right consult image/media area.
- Story / company bio: left copy block; center timeline block; right image/card block.
- Trust strip: full coded stat strip area.
- Practitioners: intro/header area; practitioner card row.
- Model: heading/copy area; model icon/process rail.
- Technology: technology card/text/media zone.
- Catalog: catalog card/product/support zone.
- Final CTA: CTA copy/button area.

No footer zone is marked because footer support art was not needed in this preview.
