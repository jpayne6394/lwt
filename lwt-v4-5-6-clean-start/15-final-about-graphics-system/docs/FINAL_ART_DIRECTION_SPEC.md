# Final Art Direction Spec

## Intent

The About page background system should read as one coordinated premium wellness-science composition, divided into page sections for later implementation.

## Approved Direction

- Overall mood: premium wellness-science, restrained, warm, calm, and credible.
- Hero: dark navy science field with subtle hex, molecule, helix, signal, orbit, and gold light accents.
- Story / company bio: warm cream continuation from Hero, using the approved Hero-to-Story interlocking signal wave.
- Lower sections: subtle continuity through motif rhythm, glow, and signal lines instead of repeating a large wave panel in every section.
- Practitioners: helix, molecule, biotech, health-science, signal, and orbit accents only. No floral.
- Trust strip: dark controlled proof-support band with no baked stats, icons, or text.
- Model: light structured systems atmosphere with organized science geometry, central glow, and subtle orbit rail. No floral.
- Technology: darker bio-signal, sensing, anatomy, and dashboard-adjacent energy. No floral.
- Catalog / supplement: warm clean catalog atmosphere. Botanical/floral accents are allowed only here.
- Final CTA: dark navy premium band with restrained gold signal flow.

## Content Exclusions

The background PNGs must not contain real page copy, cards, buttons, practitioner portraits, product bottles, stat content, footer columns, navigation, or other UI.

## Footer

No separate footer support art was exported because the approved art set ends at the Final CTA and the footer background is not needed for this local preview.
