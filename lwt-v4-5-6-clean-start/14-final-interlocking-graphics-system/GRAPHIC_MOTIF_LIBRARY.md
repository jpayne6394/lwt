# Graphic Motif Library

Use motifs consistently. A motif should support section meaning and page flow, not decorate every available surface.

## Gold Signal Wave

Purpose: primary visual energy and brand signal.

May appear:

- Hero to Story transition
- small CTA framing accent
- subtle data or pulse highlights

May not appear:

- as a giant repeated wave in every section
- behind dense text
- inside the coded Trust proof bar

## Diagnostic Orbit

Purpose: care, insight, measurement, and system intelligence.

May appear:

- Story background edges
- Technology card art
- light section corner details

May not appear:

- behind buttons or body copy
- as fake UI controls
- over practitioner cards or model labels

## Molecular Lattice

Purpose: science, nutrition, wellness intelligence, and product rigor.

May appear:

- Technology/Catalog outer background
- Catalog card internal art
- faint light-section textures

May not appear:

- as dense wallpaper
- inside the coded trust bar
- where it looks like product card structure

## Faint Navy/Blue-Gray Linework

Purpose: preserve interest in light sections without washing them out.

May appear:

- Practitioners edges
- Model background
- open fields around cards

May not appear:

- directly behind small text
- at contrast levels that make the section feel dirty or low quality

## Botanical/Vitality Linework

Purpose: living-well warmth and human vitality.

May appear:

- Story and Practitioners edges
- light background corners
- subtle Final CTA framing

May not appear:

- in a way that makes the page feel like unrelated floral wallpaper
- behind coded cards or model rail labels

## Timeline Pulse

Purpose: story progression and measured transformation.

May appear:

- Story timeline support fields
- low-detail connection into Trust

May not appear:

- as baked timeline dots or labels
- competing with real coded timeline UI

## Data Rail

Purpose: structured process, model flow, and platform intelligence.

May appear:

- Model background support
- Technology/Catalog transition
- low-contrast edge routing

May not appear:

- as a replacement for the coded model rail
- as fake navigation or fake card chrome

## Full-Body Bio-Signal Human

Purpose: Technology card feature illustration.

May appear:

- inside the Technology card internal graphic
- as a contained visual with bio-signal linework

May not appear:

- as a page-wide background figure
- behind unrelated sections
- in the Catalog card
- behind body copy or buttons

## Product/Catalog Support Linework

Purpose: support catalog intelligence without baking product UI.

May appear:

- Catalog card internal graphic
- Technology/Catalog outer background edges

May not appear:

- as fake product cards
- as fake prices, SKU labels, badges, or buttons
- in the footer
