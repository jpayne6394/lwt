# Safe Zones

Safe zones define where coded content must stay visually clean. Background art may approach these areas, but it must not create contrast problems, fake UI, or content collisions.

## Hero Text

Keep the primary hero text zone quiet. Use soft fields, low-detail gradients, or very faint linework only. No dense wave, orbit, lattice, or human figure should sit behind the headline, body copy, or buttons.

Desktop: protect the left text column from the top nav area through the primary CTA row.

Mobile: protect the full-width stacked text area near the top of the hero slice.

## Hero Right Media

The right media area is coded or media-driven, not baked into the background. Background art may frame it from edges but must not imply a fixed card, border, button, or model rail.

## Story Copy, Timeline, And Right Image

Story copy and timeline content need a calm field. The Hero to Story swoop may pass through the transition, but it should resolve before it interferes with story text.

The right image or visual block should have clean surrounding space. Do not place high-contrast linework directly behind faces, product imagery, or readable UI.

## Trust Bar Coded Area

The Trust proof bar is coded. Do not bake trust stats, labels, icons, dark panels, or a dark wavy band into the graphics. The background support should be quiet enough that coded trust content controls the visual hierarchy.

## Practitioners Cards

Practitioner cards are coded. Background art should stay around the section perimeter, between major open fields, or behind low-risk whitespace. Do not draw fake card edges, fake portraits, or decorative lines that look like card dividers.

## Model Rail

The Living Well Today Model rail is coded. Keep the rail and step labels readable. Background linework may echo the flow of the rail at low contrast, but it must not duplicate, compete with, or visually replace the coded rail.

## Technology Card

The Technology card may contain the full-body bio-signal human graphic. Treat that figure as internal illustration art, not page background. Protect card text, tags, buttons, and any coded feature elements.

## Catalog Card

Catalog support art may suggest product intelligence, catalog structure, or diagnostic linework. It must not bake product cards, fake SKUs, product titles, prices, badges, or button-like shapes.

## Final CTA Copy And Buttons

Final CTA content needs a clean central field. Graphics may frame the CTA from corners or lower edges, but should not sit behind CTA buttons or make the section feel like a repeated Hero wave.

## Footer Handoff

The final CTA bottom edge should hand off calmly to the coded footer. Do not bake footer columns, footer links, social icons, or legal text into graphics.
